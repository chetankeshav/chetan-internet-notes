## Summary

A zero-oil chicken curry made in a pressure cooker, with no added oil and no extra spices. Raw chicken, onion, crushed ginger and garlic, dry red chillies, whole potatoes and whole tomatoes are cooked together in water, then the softened vegetables are mashed back into the pot to thicken the broth. Fresh coriander leaves and green chillies are stirred in at the end, with no further cooking — that fresh finish is what the video presents as the trick that makes it so flavourful.

Note: the video does not give amounts for any ingredient, so none are listed below. The amounts for water and cooking time below come from the creator's own replies to comments on the post.

## Ingredients

- Chicken, bone-in pieces
- Onion, sliced
- Ginger and garlic, crushed
- Dry red chillies
- Potatoes, whole and peeled
- Tomatoes, whole
- Water
- Salt
- Coriander leaves, fresh
- Green chillies

No oil, and no other spices.

## Procedure

1. Put the raw chicken pieces into a dry pressure cooker. No oil.
2. Add the sliced onion, crushed ginger and garlic, dry red chillies, whole peeled potatoes and whole tomatoes.
3. Pour in water — just enough to cover the chicken.
4. Add salt.
5. Close the lid and pressure-cook for two to three whistles.
6. Open the cooker. Lift out the soft-cooked tomatoes, potatoes and chillies onto a plate, mash them, and return the mash to the pot. Stir it in — this thickens the broth into a pulpy, orange-red curry. The video cuts from the whole vegetables on the plate to the mash in the pot, so the mashing itself is not shown on camera.
7. Stir in a generous amount of fresh coriander leaves.
8. Add the green chillies and mix well.
9. Taste and serve.
