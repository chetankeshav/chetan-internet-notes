## Summary

Bhalla recommends *The Finest Hotel in Kabul* by BBC journalist Lyse Doucet as a history book told through people rather than dates. The book follows the staff of a hotel in Kabul — especially the women who worked there — as they kept showing up through decades of regime change and upheaval.

His main point is that this kind of history, focused on how people lived through a period, lands very differently from the date-and-name history most of us were taught. He rates it 10/10, and recommends it in particular to anyone studying journalism or hoping to work in hospitality.

> "Why aren't we taught history like this?"
