## Summary

A hearty, freezer-friendly vegetable soup built around pearled barley, which gives it a chewy texture and makes it very filling. It is a one-pot soup with onion, garlic, carrots, tomatoes, potato and frozen vegetables, finished with lemon juice and parsley. The recipe costs about $7.05 in total, about $1.18 a serving, and each serving is about 2 cups.

## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 lb carrots, about 4
- 1 28 oz can diced tomatoes, with juices
- 1 cup pearled barley
- 1/2 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Freshly cracked black pepper
- 6 cups vegetable broth
- 1 russet potato, about 3/4 lb
- 1 cup frozen green beans
- 1/2 cup frozen corn
- 1/2 cup frozen peas
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Fresh parsley, for garnish (optional)

## Procedure

1. Dice the onion and mince the garlic. Add them to a large soup pot with the olive oil and saute over medium heat for about 5 minutes, until the onion is soft and translucent.
2. Peel and dice the carrots. Add them to the pot, along with the diced tomatoes with their juices, the barley, basil, oregano, pepper and vegetable broth. Stir to combine.
3. Put the lid on, turn the heat up to medium-high and bring to a boil. Then turn it down to medium-low and simmer, with the lid on and stirring occasionally, for 30 minutes.
4. While it simmers, peel and dice the potato into 1/2-inch cubes. After the 30 minutes, when the barley is mostly tender, add the potato and simmer for 10 minutes more, until the potato is tender.
5. Add the frozen green beans, corn and peas, stir, and heat through for about 5 minutes.
6. Stir in the lemon juice. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper, and serve hot, with fresh chopped parsley on top if you like.

The soup freezes well. Serve it with buttered crusty bread, if you like. Other vegetables can be swapped in — celery or mushrooms at the start, kale or beans with the potato, or zucchini or spinach at the end.
